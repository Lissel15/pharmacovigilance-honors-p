# Research Journal: Weeks 38 to 39 (Vocabulary Extraction, Bayesian Classification & UMLS Normalization)
**Project:** Pharmacovigilance & Predictive Modeling for Protease Inhibitors  
**Program:** UDLAP Honors Program  
**Researcher:** Lissel Erandi Flores Polvo  

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Focus
During Weeks 38 and 39, the project combined practical data engineering tasks—such as vocabulary extraction on the pilot dataset—with advanced machine learning theory (Bayesian classification) and strategic discussions with mentors regarding medical data normalization using the UMLS framework.

---

## 🔬 Key Stages & Technical Development

### 1. Pilot Vocabulary Extraction & Text Cleaning (Week 38)
* **Pilot Implementation:** Performed vocabulary extraction and analysis using the smallest database (*Atazanavir*, 255 reports) to observe processing behavior and identify potential improvements.
* **Pipeline Refinement:** 
  * Processed unstructured clinical narratives (`Narrative and comments`) via Stanza (`mimic` + `i2b2` NER), yielding over 5,711 raw unique terms (`PROBLEM`, `TEST`, `TREATMENT`).
  * Implemented automated cleaning scripts to remove stopwords, prefixes, and punctuation, and systematically merged variants (e.g., grouping "the rash" and "rash" into a consolidated count).
  * Addressed challenges such as distinguishing drug names from clinical reactions and isolating core symptoms.

### 2. Bayesian Classification & Dual-Mentor Progress Review (Week 39)
* **Advanced Machine Learning:** Participated in mentorship classes focusing on **Bayesian classification**, examining its underlying structure, mathematical application, and algorithmic complexities.
* **Dual-Mentor Evaluation Meeting:** Presented comprehensive project updates to both research mentors.
* **Standardization Strategy (UMLS):** Initiated the formal request and authorization process for **UMLS (Unified Medical Language System)** to utilize its software and terminological resources. This aims to reduce vocabulary dimensionality by grouping medical synonyms and accounting for the diverse demographic origins of the clinical reports.

---

## 📈 Summary of Milestones (Weeks 38 – 39)
* **Data Engineering Execution:** Successful extraction and structured cleaning of the pilot narrative vocabulary.
* **Algorithmic Expansion:** Theoretical integration of Naive Bayes and analysis of algorithmic complexities.
* **Methodological Strategy:** Advancement toward formal medical data normalization via UMLS to handle textual synonyms.
