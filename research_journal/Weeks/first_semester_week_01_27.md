## 📌 Executive Summary & Scope
During this foundational first semester (covering Weeks 1 through 27), the core framework, bibliographic foundation, and methodological scope of the research project were successfully established. 

---

## 🔬 Key Stages & Methodological Development

### 1. Selection of Study Drugs & Social Impact
* **Target Group:** Protease inhibitors were selected as the primary study group for the research.
* **Social Significance:** This decision was driven by the massive social impact of these medications, as they are principal treatments utilized for patients with **HIV** and **COVID-19**. 
* **Data Classification:** All raw databases containing information on these drugs were comprehensively reviewed, organized, and classified. The active working space, task tracking, and database organization were maintained and structured via our [Notion Workspace](https://app.notion.com/p/TODOS-LOS-F-RMACOS-BASE-DE-DATOS-30b9fed165088060a966c97a44b2f033?source=copy_link).

### 2. Mentorship & Machine Learning Foundations
* Regular collaborative sessions and foundational classes were held with the assigned research mentor.
* The goal of these sessions was to build a rigorous theoretical understanding of machine learning models and how they apply to complex clinical datasets.

### 3. Literature Review & State of the Art
* Continuous bibliographic research was conducted throughout the semester to map existing methodologies in pharmacovigilance and deeply understand the pharmacological profile of protease inhibitors.
* As part of the academic coursework requirements, a **literature review article** was researched and written. This document synthesized existing predictive frameworks, standard terminologies, and current gaps in automated adverse drug reaction (ADR) detection, anchoring the technical work in solid scientific background.

## 📈 Summary of Milestones (Weeks 01 – 27)
* **Data Scoping:** Initial exploration, cleanup planning, and database inventory across protease inhibitor datasets (including the initial preparation for pilot cases like *Atazanavir*).
* **Theoretical Grounding:** Transition from general machine learning theory to clinical NLP applications.
* **Academic Deliverable:** Successful completion of the literature review paper mapping out the research methodology.
